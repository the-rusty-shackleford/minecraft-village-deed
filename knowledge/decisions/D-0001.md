# D-0001 — Village Deed joins the store as a Chunkworks mod: who a deed is for, what it covers, what it costs

Accepted 2026-09-24.

nfx wrote Village Deed 1.0.0 (MIT, `com.nfx.villagedeed`) as a Thief addon: buy a village for a
flat 45 emeralds and every player on the server is exempt from Thief's crime system inside it.
Its jar shipped in the pack as an override from the adopted base pack. Rusty, with nfx's
blessing: fold it into the store "under Chunkworks authorship (not nfx or Rusty)", "adopt our
licensing" (AGPL-3.0-or-later; the MIT notice dropped), and grow it. Rusty settled four points:

**Who is exempt: the owner and the players they name.** Rusty picked this over nfx's server-wide
immunity and over owner-only. `/deed trust <player>` and `/deed distrust <player>` keep the
roster, as the Warehouse Manager's panel does for chests; `/deed transfer <player>` hands the
village over as a clean break. A server config switch (`everyone_is_immune`, off) restores
nfx's rule for servers that want it.

**What it covers: property and livestock, never people.** Thief's only violent crime is killing
the village's animals (its `killing_protected` tags name horses, pigs, sheep, cows, chickens and
cats, and no villager); hurting or killing a villager is punished by vanilla's gossip and by the
guards, and no deed ever touched that. nfx's texts claimed the residents would "overlook
anything", which was false in both directions. Rusty's call: the deed covers what the village
owns (its buildings, stores, crops and animals), and every message, the lore and the broadcast
say that its people are not property. Rejected: keeping livestock a crime for owners, which
needs a second mixin to tell a kill from a theft at the crime choke point for a distinction
players would not expect from a deed to a farm.

**What it costs: 15 to 150 emeralds, by what the village holds.** Rusty chose the gentler of
three ranges (15 to 150; 30 to 300; 50 to 600). An appraisal counts the village
(`Surveyor` → `Census`) and prices it (`Tariff.STANDARD`, `Appraisal.of`): homes, residents and
their trade levels, workstations, bells, guards, land, storage, crafting blocks, valuable blocks
and the loot in its containers, rounded to five, within the server's floor and ceiling and
times its multiplier. The offer names the price and `/deed appraise` shows the lines; the price
offered is the price paid while the offer lives.

**Every village is purchasable, and Terralith's fortified villages are villages.** Three causes
kept nfx's 1.0.0 from selling: a villager standing between structure pieces (a road, a field),
short emeralds or a village already owned, both silent, and Terralith's fortified villages being
outside Thief's protected tag, so neither policed nor buyable. Now the village is found by piece,
then by bounding box, then as the nearest village within 48 blocks; every failed offer says why;
and which structures are villages is the village protocol's business (D-0002), where Terralith is
bound by data.

Kept from nfx: sneak-use on a villager only offers and the click confirms (Thief's emerald gift
is the same gesture), the claim keyed on the structure start's chunk, gossip cleared on purchase,
`/deed revoke` for operators, the deed item as a receipt, his item art. Mod id `villagedeed`
kept so the pack swaps the jar in place. Version 2.0.0: the immunity's meaning and the save
format changed; no claim existed on the box to migrate.
