# D-0003 — Claims bought on nfx's 1.0.0 carry over

Accepted 2026-09-24. Rusty, the evening after 2.0.0 shipped: "my villages no longer show up in
my atlas, which is a big problem."

The atlas was innocent. 2.0.0 reads the same saved file as 1.0.0 (`villagedeed_claims.dat`)
but only entries in its own layout (`Owner`, `Id`, …), and skipped every other entry in silence.
nfx's 1.0.0 wrote `Buyer`, `BuyerName`, `VillageId` (the structure start's chunk as a packed
long), `VillageName` and `BoughtAt`. On the box that file, last written at 03:10 on
2026-09-24, holds five purchases: three of Rusty's (Jdrum12: a Taiga Village and two Taiga
Fortified Villages), WAXER_01's Plains Fortified Village and OtatopMalloy's Plains Village.
Since 17:17 that day none of them owned their village: the atlas showed none, and Thief's law
treated each owner as a thief in their own village. The 2.0.0 record's "nobody had bought a
village on 1.0.0" was a belief, never checked against the file.

**The rule**: `Claims.load` reads both layouts. A 1.0.0 entry becomes its buyer's claim on
`structure:<chunk long>`, which is exactly the identity 2.0.0 gives the same village (its key is
the start chunk), named as stored, at 1.0.0's flat 45 emeralds, bought at the stored tick, with
nobody trusted (1.0.0 had no roster). 1.0.0 recorded no centre; the claim is centred on the
start chunk's middle at y 0 until `Claims.get` surveys it: the start chunk is loaded once, the
structure found through the providers, and the claim takes the structure's centre and current
name. A structure no longer there keeps the chunk's middle and is logged. Anything carried over
marks the data dirty, so the next save writes 2.0.0's layout and the file is read once more in
1.0.0's, never again.

Rejected: a one-off command to convert the file (an operator step nobody would know to run);
surveying at load (no level there); leaving the centre at y 0 (the atlas marker and the deed's
lore would point at bedrock, the very thing Magical Map 0.2.4 fixed).

Verified by a GameTest that writes a 1.0.0 entry for a planted hut plus an entry without a
buyer, loads it, checks the claim, its owner, price and tick, its unsurveyed centre, the survey's
centre and name, the round trip through 2.0.0's layout, and the dirty flag.
