# D-0006 — A purchase counts and takes everything the player carries, their bags included

Accepted 2026-09-28. Rusty tried to buy a village with his emeralds in his Backpacks+ bag and the
purchase did not see them. Rather than a Village Deed fix he asked for one change that makes
everything that sees the inventory see the bag: the Carried protocol (`minecraft-carried`, its
D-0001), which this mod now asks.

- **What counts**: `DeedPurchase.count` is `Carried.count`: the inventory's main slots, the
  offhand (2.1.1 counted only the main slots, so emeralds in the offhand did not count either)
  and every carried bag's storage cells. The offer's "you carry" line counts the same.
- **What is taken**: emeralds, then blocks of emerald, by the same `Payment` plan, each with
  `Carried.take`, which is all or nothing and takes the inventory's before the bags' (a bag is the
  reserve). The counts were read in the same call, so both takes succeed; should the second not,
  the first is handed back and nothing is charged.
- **What is given**: the change and the deed through `Carried.giveOrDrop`, where the inventory
  would put them with the bags in it (Backpacks+ D-0029's order) and only then at the player's
  feet; 2.1.1 dropped them when the inventory was full.

Carried is nested in the jar (jarJar, `[1.0,2.0)`), so the mod works with or without Backpacks+;
without it the inventory and offhand are all a player carries.

Verified by `CarriedPurchaseGameTests` with Backpacks+ 0.6.0, Carried 1.0.0 and Thief loaded on
the gametest server: a village bought with emeralds and blocks only in a worn Expedition bag (the
offer counts 113; the player is poorer by exactly the price; the bag's emeralds first, then the
plan's blocks, the bread untouched; the change and the deed handed over; nothing on the ground);
the inventory's ten and the offhand's three paid before the bag, the bag's first stack before its
second; six emeralds in all buy nothing and nothing is taken. The first two failed on 2.1.1 (the
offer counted 0; the inventory case could not afford a 15-emerald hut with 13 outside the bag and
128 in it). The nine earlier GameTests pass unchanged.
