# D-0005 — An emerald for every eight points; the ceiling is 100

Accepted 2026-09-28. Rusty: "150 emeralds is too expensive. Lets call it 100 emeralds at most for
the best villages, and a minimum of 15 emeralds for basic villages. Btw basically every village I
ever encountered was 150 emeralds so I think the village values are being artificially
overvalued for whatever reason."

## What was wrong

Nothing was miscounted. D-0001 set its rates by imagining a vanilla village, and this server's
villages are CTOV's and Terralith's, several times richer. A survey of every village the server's
world had generated (599 structure starts, found by their bells; the script is
`knowledge/operations/village-appraisals.py` in the server repository) did what `Surveyor.census`
and `Appraisal.of` do, against the world files on the box. It found:

- **574 of 599 villages at the ceiling of 150.** Their raw appraisals ran from 62 to 738, with a
  median of 295, about twice the ceiling. So the price said nothing about the village.
- **The spread was sensible.** Vanilla villages came to 62–170, CTOV small villages about
  240–300, medium about 350–440, large about 500–650, and Terralith's fortified villages about
  300. A price in proportion to the appraisal would order them rightly.
- **The raw total came from many categories:** workstations 20% (MoreVillagers' job sites are
  28% of those, and they are real job sites, in `acquirable_job_site`), storage 17%, crafting
  17%, land 14%, valuables 12% (mostly CTOV's lanterns), residents 9% and homes 8%. Trades and
  loot were nothing: an unvisited village's residents are novices, and its chests are unrolled
  loot tables.

## The rule

D-0001's rates stay, as **weights in points**. The standard tariff pays **an emerald for every
eight points** (`Tariff.EMERALDS_PER_POINT`, applied by `Tariff.scaled`) on a base of 15
emeralds, and the default ceiling is **100** (the floor stays 15). On the survey:

- Prices run from 20 to 100, with a median of 50.
- Vanilla villages come to 20–70 (median 30). CTOV villages have medians of 45 (small), 60
  (medium) and 80 (large); Terralith's fortified villages have a median of 50.
- Only the two richest villages reach the ceiling. At a seventh, twelve would; at a ninth, none
  would.

An eighth is exact in binary, so the weights times it and back again are the weights; the tests
check the arithmetic at a point an emerald.

`/deed appraise` lines are in emeralds (each line's points over eight), so they still add up to
the price before rounding.

The server's `config/villagedeed-server.toml` was written by 2.0.0 on 2026-09-24 with
`ceiling = 150`. NeoForge keeps a value a file already has, so the release edits that file to
100. The pack's override of the same file is nfx's 1.0.0 config (a flat `price = 45`). It has
been stale since 2.0.0, the server does not reread it, and it leaves the pack.

## Rejected

- **Lowering only the ceiling to 100.** Every village would then cost 100, since the median is
  already twice that.
- **Reweighting the categories** (lanterns out, workstations halved). Rusty asked about the range,
  and the weights order the villages sensibly. That stays open if a particular village's price
  looks wrong in play.
