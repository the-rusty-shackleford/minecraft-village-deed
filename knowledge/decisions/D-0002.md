# D-0002 — The village protocol: what a village is, and how a structure becomes one

Accepted 2026-09-24. Rusty, on bringing Terralith's fortified villages in: "This smells like the
perfect place to invent a protocol or something and bind it to Terralith fortified villages. I
want them one way or another, and I prefer the Rob Miller way."

nfx's mod equated "village" with "whatever Thief protects": one call into Thief's structure tag.
Adding Terralith there would have been a one-line tag edit and would have left the deed's notion
of a village a detail of another mod's data. Instead the deed mod owns a small specified
abstraction, `com.chunkworks.villagedeed.api`, and everything else (the mixin, the survey, the
claims, the commands) speaks only it.

**`VillageId(provider, key)`**: immutable identity, stable for the life of the world within one
dimension (claims are per-level saved data); RI: provider non-empty without a colon, key
non-empty; `toString`/`parse` round-trip.

**`Village`**: `id`, `name`, `bounds`, `contains`, `centre`. Spec: bounds hold every block the
village's law covers and contain the centre; `contains` is bounds membership; equal ids are the
same village. Implementations are immutable values.

**`VillageProvider`**: `id`, `at(level, pos)` (the village whose bounds hold the position),
`near(level, pos, radius)` (the nearest village whose bounds lie within the radius, for a
villager who wandered into the fields), `byId(level, id)` (the village a saved id names, empty
when its chunks are unloaded, never a guess). Spec: `at` and `byId` agree while the chunks are
loaded. **`VillageProviders`**: the ordered registry; the first provider to answer wins; a second
provider with the same id is refused.

**The built-in `structure` provider** recognises every worldgen structure in the tag
`#villagedeed:villages`; a village's key is its structure start's chunk, its bounds the start's
bounding box (roads and fields included, unlike Thief's piece-accurate check), its name from the
structure id. `at` asks for a piece at the position first, then any tagged structure whose box
holds it; `near` walks the structure references of the loaded chunks within reach.

**Binding a village mod is data**: `#villagedeed:villages` holds `#thief:protected` (vanilla and
CTOV) plus `terralith:fortified_village` and `terralith:fortified_desert_village` (optional
entries, so nothing breaks without Terralith), and this mod's datapack also adds the two to
`#thief:protected` so Thief polices them. A village mod, or a pack, adds its structure ids to
both tags and is done; a mod with villages that are not structures registers a provider.

Rejected: a tag edit alone (no abstraction; the deed mod stays coupled to Thief's data model);
POI-cluster villages for hand-built settlements (Thief only flags crimes inside protected
structures, so a deed there would exempt nothing; a provider for them can come later without
touching the rest).

Verified by the GameTests: the gametest datapack defines its own jigsaw structure
(`villagedeed_gametest:hut`), tags it into both tags, generates and registers it as worldgen
would, and every test buys, appraises and steals in it with Thief loaded.
